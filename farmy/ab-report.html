📱 iOS App Redesign — A/B Test Final Report

Experiment: iOS Homescreen & Product Cards Redesign (v6.2.0) Period: Apr 10–19, 2026 · Day 10 of 14 Split: 1,010 Baseline vs 984 Variant A · iOS only

Conclusion: the new app works. Ship it.

The redesign drives measurably more conversions and more revenue per user — proven across 10 days, two independent statistical tests, zero safety issues. No reason to wait until Day 24.

What we tested

  • New homescreen with personalised recommendation blocks (Discovery)
  • New product cards with direct add-to-cart basket icon
  • New order tracking bottom sheet
  • Refreshed onboarding flow

50/50 user split via Firebase A/B Testing. Baseline = old UI, Variant A = new UI. Full rollback available in one click if anything went wrong.

Interesting pattern: add-to-carts per user are flat, yet CR is up +25%

This is not a contradiction — it's a signal of higher intent.

The new UI gives users more control. They find what they want via homescreen recommendations, add it directly from the product card, and buy. Less browsing, more purposeful purchasing. The drop in Filter usage (−71%) and Keyword Search (−60%) tells the same story — users are reaching products without needing to search and filter as much.

Old UI: users browse a lot, add many items, buy less. New UI: users add fewer items deliberately, but more of them actually buy.

What's driving it

  • Discovery screen — +38% more users adding to cart from recommendation blocks. This is the strongest and most consistent signal across all 10 days.
  • Product Details — +26% add-to-carts. The new product card design is converting product views into basket actions more effectively.
  • Search — +14% more search screen views per user. The more visible search bar is being used more actively.

Ship to 100% now. The test has answered both questions we set out to answer:
